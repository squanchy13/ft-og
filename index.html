Outlook

The Gates of Orgrimmar Open: A Market Without a Past

World of Warcraft: Forever opens on 4 November at 3:00 p.m. PDT, which is midnight CET going into 5 November in Europe. Blizzard has confirmed there is no WoW Token, no paid character boost and no GDKP raiding. Since 13 October it can also put a Temporary Economy Hold on accounts suspected of trading gold. Guide sites report, though this is not yet verified, that Forever has no realms, just one auction house per ruleset and faction in each region. If that holds, our market, the Horde auction house of the PvE ruleset, will be far deeper than any classic realm's.

Earlier fresh Classic launches show where to look. In the first days, levelling materials such as linen cloth, copper ore, peacebloom, silverleaf and light leather, along with bags, were scarce and expensive, then got cheaper as more players reached the zones that drop them. Higher-level materials came later and also started out expensive. The first raids unlock on 9 December, so flasks, potions, food and their herbs are worth watching; in past launches they usually rose ahead of raids. My reading is that the stricter rules on gold trading will likely limit how much bought gold pushes up early prices.

The first dispatch with real figures will follow once a few days of auction house scans exist.

By our senior market analyst (Claude)

What Every Dispatch Brings

From the first scans on, each dispatch is built from the full auction house scans of our market: the Horde auction house of the PvE ruleset. Sections appear as soon as there is enough data for them.

  • Word from the Front · Game news and blue posts from the past week that can move prices, each rated by how strongly it is likely to matter and for which goods.
  • Spoils to Seize · Goods selling well below their usual price, ranked by the profit you can expect after the auction cut and deposits, with a sensible amount to buy (about one day of sales).
  • Goods to Unload · Goods selling well above their usual price: a good moment to post yours.
  • Supply Lines · Goods running short (far fewer listed than usual) or piling up (far more than usual), next to the next big event on the calendar.
  • Out of Line · Prices far outside their range of the last two weeks, often the first sign that something has changed.
  • Where the Gold Lies · The biggest markets in goods for professions, by the value of everything listed.
  • The Busiest Roads · The goods where the most gold changes hands per day, from estimated sales.
  • Ground Gained and Lost · The biggest price moves since the previous scan.
  • Quick Raids · Vendor flips: buy on the auction house and sell to a vendor, or buy from a vendor and post on the auction house.
  • Worth the Grind · Farm routes and what they pay in gold per hour at today's prices, after the auction cut.
  • The March of Prices · Price charts of the most important goods over the last two weeks.
  • Eyes on the Horizon · Goods the analyst thinks are worth watching, and why.
  • Rules of Engagement · How every signal is calculated, and where the figures come from.

How to Read a Dispatch

  • Item names · On the page, every item links to Wowhead. On Discord the name stands in a grey box: copy it and paste it straight into the auction house search.
  • Prices · In gold, silver and copper, from the cheap end of what is listed, not from a single odd listing.
  • Patience · Buy and sell signals compare today with the usual price, so they need about four days of scans. Sales estimates and two-week ranges take longer. Until then a dispatch shows the biggest markets, price moves and vendor flips.
  • Ideas, not guarantees · The market can turn by the next scan. Check the price yourself before you buy.

Dates to Mark

  • Spring 2027 · More raids and dungeons, according to Blizzard's roadmap; a Hardcore ruleset follows after launch.
